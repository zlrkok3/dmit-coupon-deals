## DMIT Plan Overview: Which Series Is Which

Three main product lines, each built around different routing strategies. The location affects latency; the tier affects route quality.

### Premium / Pro Series
Triple-carrier CN2 GIA return routing. China Telecom, Unicom, and Mobile all get premium paths. This is DMIT's flagship — lowest latency to mainland China, most stable during evening traffic surges (roughly 8–11 PM Beijing time when congestion typically peaks). No active coupon discount on these right now; they tend to sell at full rate given the demand.

### Eyeball (EB) Series
China Telecom and Unicom outbound traffic uses CN2 premium routes; China Mobile uses CMIN2. Return routes are all CMIN2. A notch below Premium performance, but the gap is smaller than the price difference. The LAX Eyeball series has the 20% recurring code active — that's real money saved every quarter if you're on annual billing.

### Tier 1 (T1) Series
Standard international routing. No specific China optimization. Priced lower than the above two, with strong bandwidth allocations. When you hit your monthly traffic cap, DMIT throttles to around 1Gbps rather than cutting you off — useful for high-volume workloads where occasional throttling beats hard cutoffs. HKG and Tokyo T1 both have solid coupon codes active right now.
